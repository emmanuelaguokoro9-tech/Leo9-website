    Choosing a TV stand

    Size the stand to your screen

    1. 01

      Go wider than the TV

      A 55-inch TV is about 122 cm wide, so choose 160 cm or more. A 65-inch TV (about 144 cm) suits 180–200 cm, and a 75-inch TV (about 166 cm) needs 200 cm or more.

    2. 02

      Plan the wall

      Floating units and wall units are fixed to the wall during installation. Tell us where your sockets and cable points are.

    3. 03

      Delivered and installed

      Our own team delivers, assembles and places the stand, or fixes the unit to your wall.
